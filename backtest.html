Read this before trusting the numbers

  • Window: a rolling 13 months of listed IPOs, so the sample is a few hundred, not thousands. Results from one market phase may not repeat in another.
  • Point-in-time: GMP filters use only the closing-day GMP. Older pages are paywalled to the last 3 readings, so they have no close-day GMP and appear only in the “apply to all” style strategies.
  • Allotment is a lottery, and the biggest unknown: without per-IPO subscription the odds are your assumption above, and net P&L scales linearly with it. Hot IPOs are the hardest to get, so a flat probability overstates the value of high-GMP filters. “Net / allotment” does not depend on the odds, so compare strategies on that first. Where final subscription is known the odds use 1/subscription (total, not retail — an approximation).
  • Exit at listing price less slippage is optimistic for SME IPOs (circuits, thin liquidity). Charges and tax are simplified (no holiday calendar, no loss carry-forward).
  • Survivorship: only IPOs that listed are included. GMP is unofficial and noisy, especially for SME. Small samples are shown with their N; do not over-read them.
