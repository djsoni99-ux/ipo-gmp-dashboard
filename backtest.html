Assumptions & download

Method and limits
  • Trade: apply at the upper band; if allotted, sell at the listing price less slippage (mainboard 0.5%, SME 2%), with STT, exchange, GST and DP charges, and STCG of 20.8% netted per financial year. Capital is blocked from the bid day to the refund (T+2) or to sale proceeds (listing + 1).
  • Allotment odds: 1 ÷ retail subscription where known, else total. Application counts are not published, so true odds may be somewhat better.
  • Decision Desk test: each IPO is scored with only the history that existed before its bid date. GMP is the closing-day GMP where available, otherwise the listing-eve GMP (counted separately; slightly optimistic). Final subscription is known only as bidding closes.
  • Samples: per-segment samples are a few hundred IPOs from one market phase, and small groups are marked. This is descriptive analytics, not investment advice.
