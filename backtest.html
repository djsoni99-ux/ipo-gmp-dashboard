Read this before trusting the numbers

  • Window: a rolling 13 months of listed IPOs. One market phase may not repeat in another, and per-segment samples are a few hundred at most.
  • Point-in-time: “GMP at close” and “first GMP” are known before bidding ends. Final subscription is known only as bidding closes (a mild look-ahead if you decide earlier). Listing-eve GMP is shown for calibration only.
  • Adverse selection is the main finding: allotment odds use 1/final subscription (total, not retail — an approximation), capped at 100%. You are most likely to be allotted in the weakest IPOs, and the hottest ones have odds near 1%. So the simple average listing gain flatters; “Gain you’d get” (odds-weighted) and expected net P&L are the honest numbers.
  • SME exits are optimistic: circuits, thin order books and market-maker quotes can stop you selling at the listing price; the default slippage is higher for SME but still a guess.
  • Survivorship & data: only IPOs that listed are included. GMP is unofficial and noisy. Charges and tax are simplified (no holiday calendar, no loss carry-forward).
