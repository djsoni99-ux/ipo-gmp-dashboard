How the score works

Each live IPO gets a 0–100 score from six factors, adapted from the IPO valuation & pricing method (comps, precedent IPOs, IPO discount, demand, bear/base/bull):

GMP signal 30 · History 20 (how similar IPOs actually listed) · Demand 20 (QIB, NII, retail — institutions first) · Valuation 15 (post-issue P/E vs recent same-sector IPOs) · Fundamentals 10 (growth, margin, ROE, leverage) · Structure 5 (fresh issue vs offer-for-sale).

Apply = score ≥ 62 and base-case listing gain ≥ 8%. Lottery = same, but allotment odds below 1 in 10, so the expected rupees are small. Consider = 45–61. Skip = weak score, base case ≤ 3%, or fewer than 45% of similar IPOs listed higher. SME calls are downgraded when the bear case is worse than −10%.

Base case blends today's GMP with what similar IPOs actually did; bear and bull use the worst and best 10% of those outcomes. Odds use retail subscription (1 ÷ times subscribed). Descriptive analytics, not investment advice.

Sources: InvestorGain (GMP, history) · Chittorgarh (financials, precedents, category subscription) · IPO Watch (second GMP).

GMP is unofficial and can vanish before listing. Calls are mechanical summaries of public data with stated weights, not advice. Verify with the RHP and exchange data before applying.
